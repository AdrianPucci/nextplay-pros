NextPlay Pros

An AI booking desk that gets overlooked athletes paid gigs, with the athlete in control of who can reach them.

https://github.com/AdrianPucci/nextplay-pros

The problem

Great stories, no way to find each other

Athletes

About 1.5% of college football players and 1% of men's basketball players reach a major pro league (NCAA). Retired, college, and rising athletes have stories, but no agent, and they're targets for "pay a fee first" scams.

Bookers

Schools, companies, nonprofits, and local businesses want a relatable athlete nearby and in budget. Bureaus focus on famous names and take 20–30% of the fee.

How the agents work

Three AI agents, code-enforced rules

The team every pro has: Scout = talent scout · Gatekeeper = booking agent & protector · Story Builder = promoter. The AI understands, ranks, writes, and judges; Python decides who exists, the budget, and who sees contact details. All on gpt-6-luna.
